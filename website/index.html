macOS · Windows · free and open source

Plays everything.
Built from scratch.

Reel opens nearly every film, show and song you have, from 4K HEVC to DTS-HD Master Audio, and adds a library, find in dialogue and lossless clip export. Every container and every decoder is written from scratch in Tessel, with no FFmpeg inside.

Download See what it does

Picks up where you left off

The home screen shows what you were watching, how much is left, and the next episode of each show. Reel remembers every file's position, audio and subtitle tracks, volume, speed, delays, picture settings and equalizer.

  • Start over or continue, your choice each time
  • The next episode starts on its own at the end of this one
  • Recently added files from your library folders

Your library, sorted for you

Point Reel at your folders. Films go under Movies, episodes under their show and season with watched marks and progress, and music under artist and album. History and playlists sit alongside.

  • M3U, PLS, XSPF, WPL and CUE playlists
  • Search, sort, grid or list, mark watched or unwatched

Find any line of dialogue

Search every subtitle track for a word, see each match with the lines around it, and jump straight there. Or open the transcript, which follows along as the film plays.

  • SRT, ASS/SSA, WebVTT, MP4 text, Blu-ray PGS and DVD subtitles
  • A second subtitle track at the top, in a style you choose
  • Text encoding detected, Chinese, Japanese and Korean included
  • Search and download from OpenSubtitles

Sound and picture, your way

A 10-band equalizer with presets, loudness normalisation for quiet dialogue and loud explosions, a compressor and limiter, and headphone crossfeed. For the picture: brightness, contrast, colour, crop, aspect ratio, zoom, rotation, sharpening and denoising.

  • Black bars detected and cropped
  • Interlaced TV and DVDs deinterlaced automatically
  • Audio and subtitle delay, speed from 0.25× to 4× with the pitch kept

Cut a clip without losing quality

Pick a start and an end, and Reel writes that part to MKV or MP4 without re-encoding, in seconds. Or make a GIF of it.

  • Choose which audio and subtitle tracks go in
  • Start on the exact frame, or at the keyframe before

Seek previews

Hover over the seek bar to see the picture at that time.

Scene strip

Thumbnails of every scene, and chapters for files that have none.

Frame by frame

Step forwards and backwards one frame at a time.

A-B loop and bookmarks

Repeat a passage, or mark moments to come back to.

Network streams

HLS, DASH and plain web addresses, with a quality menu.

DVD and Blu-ray

Disc folders and .iso images, with titles, angles and languages.

Sleep timer

Stop after a while, or at the end of what's playing.

Mini player

A small window that stays out of the way.

Your shortcuts

Every action has a key you can change.

Written entirely in Tessel

Every part of Reel, from the MKV reader and the HEVC decoder to the equalizer and the interface, is written in Tessel, a programming language for native desktop apps. No FFmpeg, no native media libraries, one small program.

Reel is free software under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later; the source code is on GitHub.
